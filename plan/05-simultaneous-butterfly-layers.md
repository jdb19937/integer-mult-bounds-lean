# 5. Fast simultaneous synthetic butterfly layers (§5, §11, CrocSwap)

Part of the [plan](../PLAN.md). The completed Mathlib host route is recorded in
[part C](C-mathlib-end-to-end.md).

## Native-controller retirement

The superseded native CompactComplex controller and its unused dependent
wrappers were removed: 635 modules and 109,973 lines. Two similarly named
modules used by the completed proof remain. The full former plan, including
its controller invariants and integration descriptions, is recoverable from
commit `ce0fac83`; see the [retirement record](../docs/retired-compact-complex.md)
and its exact manifest.

This catalog now lists surviving supporting components and unreplaced native
interfaces. It does not mark the retired controller's milestones complete or
claim the stronger compact-control exponent. The independent host proof and
its dependencies are retained and validated.

## Surviving sub-problems

### Packed arithmetic and fixed control gadgets

- Math milestone: `IntegerMultBounds.Machine.BinaryPackedControls.packed_controls` — From the original q/b/n headers, both the early and later packed control actions send every address to the cell holding exactly packedEarly and packedLate of its radix-2^q/2^b inputs. Combines `BinaryPackedEarlyCorrect.packed_entry`, `BinaryPackedLatePermutation.run_fields`, `BinaryPackedLateData.good_value`, `CountedPackedLateRun.runs_value`.
- Tape milestone: `IntegerMultBounds.Machine.BinaryPackedControlsPlaced.runs_linear` — One placed fixed machine computes both early and later packed control words in place on arbitrary caller ports from the original headers, retains caller tapes, returns its workspace blank, and runs within C times the array volume (times the certified width factor). Combines `BinaryPackedEarlyRunPlaced.runs`, `BinaryPackedEarlyRunBudget.uniform_bound`, `CountedPackedLatePlacement.late_hoare`, `CountedPackedLateRun.runs_guarded`.
